Privacy Policy

JEE Main Exam Test (package: com.wyrix.etest) by WYRIX
Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: the app works offline, has no account or sign-up, and does not collect, send or share any personal information. Your practice progress stays on your phone.

1. Who we are

JEE Main Exam Test (“the app”) is an Android app published by WYRIX (“we”, “us”). It offers practice questions, timed tests, solutions and solution PDFs for entrance exam preparation. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, so no usage data is sent to us or to any third party.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your settings and progress, the app saves a small amount of data in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your chosen theme (dark or light) and music volume
  • The exam you last selected
  • Your practice statistics: questions attempted, correct answers and your best score

This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Internet use

All questions, solutions and PDFs are included inside the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send your data over the internet.

5. Saving and sharing PDFs

When you tap the option to save or share a solution PDF, the app places a copy of that PDF in its temporary storage and opens Android’s share sheet. You choose where the file goes, for example Files, Drive or WhatsApp. We never receive the file or any information about where you send it.

6. Study music

Study music is generated on your device while you use the app. Nothing is streamed or downloaded.

7. Third-party services

The app does not use third-party services that collect data. It includes an open-source PDF viewer library (PDF.js), which runs entirely on your device.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is meant for students preparing for entrance exams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to delete or hand over for any user.

9. Content notice

The app is an independent practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the National Testing Agency (NTA), the IITs, UPSC or any other exam authority. The questions are practice material and are not official exam papers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding a feature that needs an account or an internet service, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change reaches users.

11. Contact
